定金政策

Deposit

预约需支付定金 $20 CAD 以锁定档期。已充值会员无需另付定金，费用直接从余额扣除。

A $20 CAD deposit is required to lock in your appointment. Members with a prepaid balance don't need a separate deposit — the cost is deducted directly.

仅在提交预约申请并收到定金后，预约才算正式成功；若 2 小时内未支付，系统将自动释放该时间段。

Your booking is only confirmed once the deposit is received; if unpaid within 2 hours, the slot is automatically released.
